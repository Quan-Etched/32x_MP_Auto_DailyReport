Every stage a unit passes through, from bare silicon to a packed rack. A stage is green when it produces a yield; grey when it builds or moves something without testing it. Where this dashboard has the numbers they are printed in the box, so the chart doubles as a map of what is and is not measured here.

Redrawn from the line's own flowchart (updated 20 Aug 2026) with the stages this dashboard measures joined to it. The yields cover this week from the station controllers — pega2 for VBB, pega6 for TIM, pega3 for the module stations, pega4 for L10, pega5 for L11 — counted one row per unit, not one per fixture. WST, FT and SLT sit with Sigurd and are not collected here; their boxes say so rather than showing a blank.

Two things on the line's chart are drawn here as one box, deliberately. The five SMT / ICT boards (BB, HPB, PV1, VBB, PDB) are one box because none of them reports to a controller — five boxes with no number between them would be five times the width and none of the information. And 4U ASSY and 2U ASSY share one box carrying both tags, because this chart has one column per lane and they are two parallel paths through the same one. The line's own chart draws them apart, and it is the better drawing; splitting them here needs the layout to handle branches, which it does not yet.
