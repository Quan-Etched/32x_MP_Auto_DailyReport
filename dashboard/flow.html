Every stage a unit passes through, from bare silicon to a packed rack. A stage is green when it produces a yield; grey when it builds or moves something without testing it. Where this dashboard has the numbers they are printed in the box, so the chart doubles as a map of what is and is not measured here.

Redrawn from the line's own flowchart (updated 15 Aug 2026) with the stages this dashboard measures joined to it. The yields are the last 30 days from the station controllers — pega2 for VBB, pega3 for the module stations, pega4 for L10, pega5 for L11 — counted one row per unit, not one per fixture. WST, FT and SLT sit with Sigurd and are not collected here; their boxes say so rather than showing a blank.
