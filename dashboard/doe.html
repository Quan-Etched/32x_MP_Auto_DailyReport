All-hands view →

Pass and fail could not carry the question. A module that failed once and passed on the retest and one that failed and is still on a shelf were both “fail”, and they are opposite situations — the first cost a fixture slot, the second cost a module. So every unit gets exactly one of four outcomes at each station, and the fourth is the absence of a verdict rather than a verdict of its own. MLT and HTT only — TIM is a bake, its repeats are a different animal, and it is reported in its own column elsewhere.

MLT week over week

The same four outcomes as a share of each week, with the share of first-attempt failures that came back beside it.

One outcome per unit per station. Pass means the first attempt in the week passed. Retest Pass means the first attempt failed and a later one passed — the unit is back in the production flow. Bonepile means the first attempt failed and the unit has never passed: no retest at all, or every retest failed too. No result means it ran and produced no verdict, which is rare and is kept separate because a unit with no result is not a pass and blaming the module for it would be worse.

Fail is not a fifth bucket. It is Retest Pass plus Bonepile — every unit whose first attempt failed. The four exclusive outcomes add up to the units that ran; Fail is the part of that total the line usually means when it says the word.

Counted over the units that ran in the week, which is a slightly wider population than the first-pass yield on the weekly tracker: that one counts units new to the station, so the two percentages differ by a fraction. Both are stated where they appear rather than reconciled by hand.
