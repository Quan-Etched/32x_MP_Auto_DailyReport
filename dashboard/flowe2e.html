Every insertion on the line, including the ones the other two charts fold into a neighbour: Flash and BFT as separate tests, the CK and PDB checkpoints, and the 2U test named for the level it runs at. A stage is green when it produces a yield; grey when it builds or moves something. Where this dashboard has the numbers they are in the box, and where it does not the box says so.

This is the chart to read when the question is what is actually measured, because it is the one where an unmeasured stage gets its own box instead of hiding inside a neighbour's. Three of them: BFT — I2C, UART, GPIO, power-on sequence, PRBS (ETCH-39584) — reports to no controller, and drawn together with Flash it shared the VBB provisioning yield, which lent a number to a test nobody measures. CK and PDB are checkpoints on the way into assembly and are not collected either.

Flash is ROT OTP, the production certificate and the bootloader lock, which is what this repo's VBB provisioning runs are — so that box, and only that box, carries the number. The 2U test appears twice because it runs twice: 2U Component at the 2U/4U stage and 2U System at 6U, one station key behind both, and each box says whose figure it is showing.

Yields cover this week from the station controllers — pega2 for VBB, pega6 for TIM, pega3 for the module stages, pega4 for L10, pega5 for L11 — counted one row per unit, not one per fixture. WST, FT and SLT sit with Sigurd and are not collected here.
